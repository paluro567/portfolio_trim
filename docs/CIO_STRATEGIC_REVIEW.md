# CIO Strategic Review — Will Version 4 Become the Platform We Set Out to Build?

**Sponsor's review.** Not an engineering assessment. The question is whether the architecture we are
about to fund arrives at the product that was actually asked for.

---

### What was intentionally deferred, and whether it weakens the objective

| Deferred | Why | Preserves or weakens? |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence contribution > 0 | No source has demonstrated reliability | **Preserves.** Admitting unvalidated evidence is what produced the +18–26 pp overclaim |

**The deferrals are honest and individually correct. Collectively they produce a platform that is
roughly 40% of the stated vision and gives no signal that it has stopped short.** That asymmetry — each
deferral defensible, the aggregate materially short — is the substance of this review.

## PART 2 — What product are we actually building?

If V4 ships exactly as specified today:

> ### A tax-aware, policy-driven portfolio exposure-management system with institutional-grade decision explanation, and a correctly-shaped but empty research apparatus.

**Precisely what it is:**

- **Not a portfolio management platform.** No execution, no order management, no custody integration.
- **Not a reporting system.** It makes decisions; reports are its output, not its purpose.
- **Not a quantitative research platform.** The research spine exists — PIT store, snapshots, contracts,
  ledger — and produces nothing, because nothing is admitted.
- **It is a policy engine with an explanation layer and a dormant research spine.**

The most useful framing for a sponsor: **V4 is the chassis of the intended vehicle, built to the right
specification, with the engine bay correctly shaped and empty.**

That is not a criticism. A chassis is what you build first. But a chassis drives — badly, slowly, and
usefully enough that you may stop noticing there is no engine.

### What it will genuinely do well on day one

Tell you that a position is 13.4 points over your stated cap; that trimming it realises $1.2M of gain
against $60k of budget; that a full exit takes 0.02 days of volume; exactly what would reverse the
recommendation; what changed since last review and why; and — where it has no view — say so explicitly
rather than manufacturing one.

**That is more than this project has ever produced, and less than was asked for.**

## PART 4 — Has the Norgate question changed?

### Yes. From the sponsor's chair, it changes materially.

Every earlier review in this file answered a narrower question — *"should we buy it now?"* — and
answered correctly: **no**, because it did not address the measured bottleneck and the instrument's
sensitivity was unknown.

**The CIO question is different: is it required to reach the destination?**

## **REQUIRED LATER — and it is on the critical path, not optional.**

The reasoning is short and I do not think it is escapable:

1. The vision requires **evidence-backed** recommendations.
2. Evidence affects direction only at **DIRECTIONAL tier**.
3. DIRECTIONAL tier requires demonstrated **out-of-sample directional reliability**.
4. Directional reliability cannot be certified on **survivor-only outcomes** — the estimate is biased in
   a known direction and the bias is not estimable from the data.
5. Therefore: **without survivorship-clean data, no source can ever reach DIRECTIONAL, and the platform
   is a policy engine permanently.**

Four earlier reviews were right that it was premature. **None of them was entitled to conclude it was
optional, and I am correcting that here.**

### The earliest milestone at which it becomes scientifically justified

Two conditions, both necessary:

> **A.** The **MDE sweep** shows the pipeline can detect an effect of realistic size (IC ≈ 0.03–0.05) at
> an attainable universe. *If it cannot, no dataset helps and the purchase is never justified.*
>
> **B.** At least **one evidence source has cleared descriptive reliability** to ADVISORY tier.
> *Buying clean outcome data to test a source that has not shown it accurately describes what it claims
> is buying a better ruler for an object we have not confirmed exists.*

**Condition B has never been stated before in this project and I regard it as the more important of the
two.** It is free, it has never been attempted, and it prevents the failure mode of buying data to test
candidates that would fail a cheaper test first.

**Trigger, stated so it can be checked:** *purchase when a candidate source has earned everything free
evidence can earn, and the only remaining question is whether it predicts returns.*

On the V4 roadmap that is after P7 (ledger operational) plus the MDE work — roughly **month 9–11**, not
month 1 and not never.

## PART 5 — The roadmap from V4 to the vision

V4's P0–P9 deliver the chassis. What follows is what has never been planned.

### V5 · Descriptive reliability era — 3 months · **free**

**Objective:** find out whether any source accurately describes the state it claims.
**Evidence required:** none. Measurable today.
**Engineering:** descriptive scoring in the ledger; instrument 3–5 candidate sources at SHADOW.
**Data:** existing free data. Sufficient.
**Scientific gate:** **≥1 source reaches CONTEXT; ≥1 reaches ADVISORY.**
**Exit:** the ledger is no longer empty. If nothing clears CONTEXT, the vision is unreachable and the
product is formally redefined here — at three months and zero dollars.

### V6 · Instrument calibration — 2 months · **free**

**Objective:** measure what the pipeline can actually detect.
**Engineering:** staged positive control — inject known IC at score level, feature level, and via a
documented real anomaly; sweep effect size × breadth × years × horizon.
**Scientific gate:** **an MDE surface exists.** If IC = 0.03 is undetectable at every attainable
configuration, **stop** — the vision is unreachable at any data budget.
**Exit:** every historical null becomes interpretable, and the data purchase becomes exactly priceable.

### V7 · Clean data + first directional test — 4 months · **$600–2,000**

**Objective:** determine whether any source predicts returns out of sample.
**Evidence required:** V5 gate passed **and** V6 gate passed. **Both.**
**Data:** survivorship-clean outcomes, ~200–500 names × ≥20 years, delisting returns with ≥80% fill.
**Scientific gate:** **≥1 source reaches DIRECTIONAL, or a decisive negative.**
**Exit — and this is the branch point of the whole programme:**
- **Positive** → proceed to V8. The vision is reachable.
- **Decisive negative** → **V8–V10 never happen.** The platform is permanently what V4 delivers, and
  that is now a *finding*, not a disappointment.

### V8 · Horizon differentiation — 4 months

**Objective:** make 1W and 1Y genuinely different recommendations rather than one decision shown five
times.
**Evidence:** V7 positive, at ≥2 horizons.
**Gate:** cross-horizon differences attributable to *evidence*, not only to decision rules.

### V9 · Vision completion — 6–9 months

Market/regime context · rebuilt embargoed historical behaviour · PIT fundamentals (separate vendor) ·
confidence calibration with real content.
**Each subsystem enters at SHADOW and earns its tier. No exceptions, no fast-tracking.**
**Gate:** ≥4 sources at ADVISORY or above, spanning ≥2 information families.

### V10 · The platform as envisioned — 12 months of operation

**Gate:** all nine supporting elements live, each with earned reliability; terminal-wealth disclosure
reporting real history; ≥1 completed promotion *and* ≥1 completed demotion — proving the ledger works
in both directions.

### Cumulative

|---|---|---|

## PART 6 — CIO memo

### *"If this architecture is implemented exactly as proposed, will it ultimately become the platform I originally envisioned?"*

# Not on its own. It will become the chassis, faithfully. Whether it becomes the vehicle depends on a question the architecture deliberately refuses to prejudge.

**Why the architecture is right anyway:** every element of the vision that does not depend on predictive
skill — the actions, the portfolio context, the explanation, the honest confidence, the falsifiability —
V4 delivers **in full and better than asked for**. The "why not exit?" trace alone is something most
institutional platforms cannot produce. And it delivers all of it *without* requiring a single
validated signal, which is why it can ship at all.

### Where the divergence begins

Not at the DIRECTIONAL gate. **The divergence begins at principle P1 — *correct at zero signal*.**

That principle is simultaneously the architecture's greatest strength and the exact mechanism by which
the vision can quietly never arrive. It makes evidence *optional*. Optional subsystems in a working
system do not get built, because nothing breaks without them.

**The safeguard and the risk are the same design decision.** I would not remove it — removing it would
reproduce V1 — but I want it named, because it means the vision will not arrive by momentum. It will
arrive only if it is *scheduled and gated*, which is what Part 5 exists to do.

### The single highest-risk strategic mistake

> ## **That V4 succeeds.**

Not that it fails. A working policy engine with excellent explanations will be genuinely useful. It will
answer the daily question. It will feel like the project arrived.

And it will remove every source of pressure that would otherwise drive the evidence programme. There is
no failing test, no complaint, no visible gap. Years pass. The platform is good. **It is not the
platform that was asked for, and nothing in the system will ever say so.**

This is the most likely way to spend three years building the wrong product, and it is more dangerous
than any technical risk in the file, because every technical risk announces itself.

### The countermeasure

A standing quarterly review with **one** question:

> **"How many evidence sources have earned ADVISORY or DIRECTIONAL tier since the last review?"**

- **Zero for two consecutive quarters** → the V5/V6 gates are formally re-examined.
- **Zero for four consecutive quarters** → **the vision is declared unreachable and the product is
  formally redefined** as what it actually is: a policy and exposure-management engine with
  best-in-class explanation.

That is not pessimism. It is the only mechanism that converts silent drift into an explicit decision —
and given that this project has now deferred its own validation study three times, silent drift is the
failure mode with the strongest historical evidence behind it.

### Decision

**Fund V4 as specified.** It is the correct chassis and it is honestly built.

**Schedule V5 and V6 now, dated, before V4 completes** — they are free, they gate everything, and they
are the only work that can tell us whether the destination exists.

**Hold the data purchase until both gates pass.** Then buy without further debate: at that point it is
on the critical path, and hesitating would be the mirror-image error of buying too early.
