## §E — Master script

### E.1 Opening *(verbatim)*

> "Thank you for the time. I'm evaluating some portfolio report formats. I did not design them and I have
> no stake in which one performs better.
>
> I'll show you several positions from a portfolio. For each one I'll ask what you would do and why —
> your normal judgement, no right answers. Then I'll show you some additional material and ask you again.
> Sometimes your view will change and sometimes it won't. **Both are equally useful to me** — a format
> that doesn't change anything is exactly what I need to find out.
>
> Please think aloud. If something is unclear, confusing, or not worth reading, say so plainly — that is
> the most useful thing you can tell me. Any questions before we start?"

Then hand over `00_frame_consent.md` pages 1–3. Obtain the signature.

### E.1b Warm-up *(practice only — PER AMENDMENT 001, A-001)*

Run `01_participant/warmup.md`. **Its moderator script is on that sheet and is read verbatim.**

- Separate portfolio (Kestrel) and security (DELTA). **PRE form only — no POST form.**
- Target 3:00, hard stop 4:00.
- Mark the form **`WARM-UP — DISCARD`** and set it aside. It enters no dataset.
- Do not comment on the investment answer. Do not mention that later cases have additional material.

### E.2 Commit phase *(verbatim, every case)*

> "Here's a position and the relevant portfolio context. Take as long as you need. When you're ready:
> **what would you do with this position, and roughly how much?**"

*[record the verbal answer]*

> "Please write that on the form — action, size, confidence 1 to 5, and in one line the main reason."

**► CONFIRM THE PRE FORM IS COMPLETE. INITIAL IT. ONLY THEN CONTINUE. ◄**

### E.3 Reveal phase *(verbatim; cases A, B, C, D, E, G only)*

> "Here's some additional material on the same position. Read it however you normally would. Tell me
> when you're done."

Say nothing further. Do not explain any section.

### E.4 Case F distractor *(verbatim; Case F ONLY — start a 3:00 timer)*

> "Before you finalise — something unrelated. How often do you normally review positions like this one?"
>
> *[listen]* "And what usually prompts a review outside that cadence?"
>
> *[listen]* "Who else, if anyone, sees it?"

**At exactly 3:00, stop and proceed to E.5.** Do not mention that no material was shown.

### E.5 Re-elicit *(verbatim, every case)*

> "Now: **what would you do with this position, and roughly how much?**"

*[record]* → "Please complete the second form."

**► THE REASONING FIELD (Q3) IS REQUIRED ON EVERY CASE, INCLUDING WHEN NOTHING CHANGED. ◄**

### E.6 Probes *(only after the POST form is complete)*

- "Talk me through how you got to that."
- *if changed:* "What specifically led you there?" → *if vague:* "Can you point to the part of the page?"
- *if unchanged:* "Was there anything there you'd have acted on in a different situation?"
- "Was anything on that page not worth your time?"

### E.7 Final questionnaire

Hand over `forms.md` FORM 3. For question 5, place **Case E page 2** and **Case G page 2** side by side.

### E.8 Debrief *(verbatim)*

> "That's everything — thank you.
>
> To be transparent about what this was: I was measuring whether reports like these change the decisions
> people actually intend to make. One of the cases deliberately had no report attached, as a comparison.
> Nothing you said was right or wrong, and I wasn't hoping for any particular answer.
>
> I'll send the $100 today. I'll share what we find once all sessions are done — that'll be a few weeks.
> Please don't discuss the specifics with anyone who might take part.
>
> Anything you want to ask me now that we're done?"
