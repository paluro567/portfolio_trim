## 3. DEFERRED — and why. New blocker **B-07**

**A-2026-001 (Arm 1 recovery / MDE₉₀) and the numeric half of A-2026-003 (MDE₈₀) were applied, then REVERTED from the archive.**

On verification against the archived original, the amendments' stated "originally published" values **do not match the published artifact** for 2 of 3 cells:

| 1y target IC | **Actually published** | A-2026-001 claims published | Match |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.12 | 0.325 | 0.325 | ✔ |
| 0.15 | **0.405** | 0.340 | ✘ |
| 0.40 | **0.915** | 0.900 | ✘ |

Consequence: published recovery at IC 0.40 is **0.915 ≥ 0.90**, so the published `mde_90 = 0.40` is **internally consistent with the published curve**. A-2026-001's core correction — "1y MDE₉₀ falls to *not achieved* because recovery drops 0.900 → 0.895" — rests on a baseline that is not the published one.

The same applies to A-2026-003's MDE₈₀ figures (3m 0.25 → 0.20, 1y 0.40 → 0.30): the published curve yields 0.25 and 0.40 self-consistently.

**Root cause:** the audit re-executed the extended grid (IC ≥ 0.12) at 400 bootstrap draws, whereas the published file was generated by `v6_arm1b.py` at 300 draws with an independent seed stream. The audit's numbers are therefore an **independent coherent re-run**, not a correction of an error in the published file. This is A-2026-003's own provenance defect manifesting a second time.

**Action taken:**
- `V6_ARM1_RECOVERY_CURVES.csv` — restored from archive; **provenance columns only** applied; every `recovery_prob` left at its published value.
- `V6_ARM1_MDE_TABLE.csv` — restored from archive; published `mde_80` / `mde_90` **preserved unchanged**; the audit's re-run values recorded **alongside** in new columns `audit_rerun_mde_80`, `audit_rerun_mde_90`, `audit_rerun_note`.

Nothing was overwritten. Both readings are now visible in the same artifact.

**B-07 (open):** amendments A-2026-001 and the numeric half of A-2026-003 must be re-derived against the actual published baseline, or formally reissued as an independent re-run rather than a correction. **This requires re-running Arm 1's extended grid, which this task is not authorized to do.**
