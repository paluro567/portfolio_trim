### 2 · The CIO rule — applied mechanically

| Consecutive quarters at zero | Action | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **0–1** | Continue. Record and proceed | Program Execution Director |
| **2** | **RE-EXAMINE THE GATES AND RESEARCH PROCESS.** Written analysis required: are the criteria unreachable, or is the research not reaching them? | Program Execution Director, reviewed by Sponsor |
| **3** | Escalate. Sponsor is formally notified in writing. Prepare the redefinition option | Sponsor notified |
| **4** | **DECLARE THE EVIDENCE-BACKED VISION UNREACHABLE, OR FORMALLY REDEFINE THE PRODUCT** | **SPONSOR (CIO) — cannot be delegated** |

> **The four-quarter decision is reserved to the sponsor and may not be delegated, deferred, or
> satisfied by a plan to try harder.** A plan is not a promotion.

### 5 · Drift indicators — three questions with binary answers

```
  D1  Has any V4 production code been written without G1 PASS?        ▢ no  ▢ YES -> VIOLATION
  D2  Has any tier cap been raised without a governance event?        ▢ no  ▢ YES -> VIOLATION
  D3  Has any data been purchased without all four G6 conditions?     ▢ no  ▢ YES -> VIOLATION

  Any YES is a program-integrity breach and is reported to the sponsor immediately.
```

### 7 · Redefinition memo — required contents at four quarters

If the sponsor redefines the product, the memo must state:

1. **What the platform actually is** — on the evidence, a policy-and-explanation engine.
2. **Which vision elements are formally abandoned**, itemised against the original nine.
3. **What evidence would reopen the question** — a specific, falsifiable trigger.
4. **What happens to V5/V6/V7 work** — archived, continued at reduced scope, or stopped.
5. **The residual value delivered** — stated honestly, neither inflated nor dismissed.

> A redefinition is a **result**, not a failure. The programme was designed to reach exactly this
> decision cheaply if the evidence does not arrive. Reaching it at month 12 for under $800 is a
> **successful outcome of the governance design.**
